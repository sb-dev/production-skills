# Five-Book Bootstrap Research Foundation

**Status:** Accepted family process decision  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 10 September 2026

## 1. Decision and scope

Adopt **Seed → Five → Challenge** for newly generated Production Skills bootstraps. Establish a complementary five-book foundation, extract traceable production knowledge from directly examined material, then challenge and extend it through broader domain research before encoding capabilities.

User-provided books remain in the foundational corpus unless the user explicitly approves their removal or replacement. Selecting complementary books for empty slots does not need additional approval unless separately required by the user.

This is a normative bootstrap policy accepted through the family-design review, not a claim that five is an empirically optimal number of books or that a five-book corpus exhausts a discipline. Its expected benefit is deeper, complementary and more traceable domain research. That benefit has not been benchmarked by this documentation change.

Affected work: the central bootstrap recipes, the project-contract lifecycle overview, and domain bootstraps generated after adoption. Existing domain repositories and their current execution branches are not modified by this decision.

## 2. Evidence and design rationale

The [Business Building Skills bootstrap, version 1.1](https://github.com/sb-dev/business-building-skills/blob/main/docs/research-logs/2026-09-08-business-building-skills-new-project-bootstrap-process.md), reviewed on 10 September 2026, is the reference design. Its book-derived knowledge rule requires capabilities rather than book-specific skills. Its Stage 2 extracts and reconciles five books; Stage 3 challenges that corpus through broader professional and empirical practice.

That specification demonstrates a useful separation of responsibilities. It is not evidence that the stages have been executed, that all book claims are correct, or that its particular reading list should be copied across domains.

The family [bootstrap generation guide](../bootstrap/README.md) already selects progressive examples for combined capability coverage rather than individual impressiveness. Apply the same selection principle to books: select the most useful complementary set for the domain's intended outcomes.

The existing [project contract](../specs/02-production-skills-project-contract.md) requires durable research logs, domain-owned workflows, self-contained skills and evidence-based maturity. The new method extends research preparation while preserving those product responsibilities.

The reviewed alternatives were books-first, research-first and parallel evidence streams. Seed → Five → Challenge was selected because bounded reconnaissance improves book selection, direct extraction provides a coherent provisional model, and broader independent research can challenge that model and identify missing responsibilities. Reconnaissance is bounded to avoid duplicating the full research stage.

## 3. Accepted requirements

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Corpus | Exactly five distinct foundational books; the wider bibliography is unrestricted. |
| Selection | Derive domain coverage first; compare a broader candidate pool for combined contribution, depth, credibility, perspective, durability and access. |
| Overlap | Preserve useful depth or contrasting methods; recommend replacement only for a material improvement. Do not invent numeric overlap scores. |
| Provided books | Require explicit approval before removal, replacement or demotion to supplementary reading. Record gain, loss, rationale and decision. |
| Pending decisions | Silence is not approval. Unresolved required permission blocks selection completion. |
| Declined changes | Retain the book and address residual gaps through remaining selections and broader research. |
| Access | Distinguish availability from actual examination; record edition, locations, reading scope and limitations. |
| Extraction | Examine all five meaningfully for their intended contributions; secondary summaries, model memory and bibliographies are not substitutes. |
| Synthesis | Reconcile overlap and conflict into provisional capabilities, not one skill per book. Source membership does not imply claim acceptance. |
| Challenge | Investigate support, criticism, failure conditions and knowledge missing from the books against independent domain coverage. |
| Evidence | Distinguish supported findings, qualified methods, heuristics and unresolved claims. Do not call all retained knowledge validated. |
| Completion | Selection, extraction and challenge are standalone stages with distinct gates and committed substantive outputs. |
| Publication | Publish independently expressed synthesis and references; do not redistribute supplied books or substantial copied content without permission. |
| Runtime | Use concise relevant skill references, not a requirement to load five books or the full bootstrap history. |

## 4. Ownership and implementation

The detailed rules are owned by [domain-research-process.md](../bootstrap/domain-research-process.md). The [new-project process](../bootstrap/new-project-process.md) owns stage progression, dependencies and exit criteria. The [generation guide](../bootstrap/README.md) requires custom bootstraps to preserve the method while distinguishing planned stages from completed evidence.

The project-contract lifecycle, root README, documentation index and research-log guide link or summarise the method rather than maintaining competing detailed contracts. Domain-specific reading lists, approval decisions, extraction and challenge findings remain in the owning domain's `docs/research-logs/`.

No new shared runtime, ontology, database, scoring framework, book-shaped skill hierarchy or mandatory runtime reading corpus is introduced. The five-book rule is a research process requirement, not a cross-domain production abstraction.

## 5. Migration and preservation

Apply the revised recipes when generating a new domain bootstrap. Do not bulk-rewrite existing domain specifications or automatically change books, stage numbers, completed research, execution branches or maturity labels.

An existing project may adopt the method through an explicit migration decision in its owning repository. Review existing evidence, reuse adequate work, identify missing requirements, and record supplementary stages or a stage mapping without erasing historical identifiers. Supplied-book substitutions still need specific approval; adopting the family method does not authorise unspecified replacements.

The canonical family process now has selection, extraction and challenge as Stages 2, 3 and 4. Later family stages move by two positions, including production scaffolding at Stage 13. These numbers apply to the revised family recipe, not automatically to any existing domain bootstrap.

Retain historical domain bootstrap files already held centrally. New target repositories are created before substantive bootstrap research; their own research-log directories hold their bootstrap specifications and execution evidence.

The six canonical specification responsibilities, 5×3 progressive examples, Extension Packs, evaluation, installation validation, optional Pactwright boundary and maturity semantics are unchanged.

## 6. Review scenarios

These scenarios define expected behaviour for reviewing the policy. They are not claims that a book-selection agent or a migrated domain implementation has passed a benchmark.

| Scenario | Required result |
|---|---|
| No books supplied | Research candidates and select a complementary set of five. |
| Two complementary books supplied | Retain both and select the best complementary three. |
| Supplied books overlap but add useful depth | Retain them unless a materially better portfolio justifies proposing a change. |
| Replacement approved | Apply the specified replacement and preserve the decision record. |
| Replacement declined | Retain the book and cover remaining gaps elsewhere. |
| Replacement unanswered | Keep it pending; do not complete selection or silently demote the supplied book. |
| More than five books supplied | Ask permission for the proposed foundational five and exclusions. |
| Selected book unavailable | Record the access need; obtain source material or resolve a proposed alternative before extraction completion. |
| Only excerpts available | Analyse only the contribution supported by the examined context; disclose coverage and do not claim a full-book review. |
| Books agree but repeat one underlying claim | Seek independent evidence; do not count repetition as corroboration. |
| Important knowledge absent from all five | Research the gap independently; the foundational corpus is not the whole bibliography. |
| A supplied book's claim is unsupported | Qualify or reject the claim without needing to remove the book. |
| Existing project already mid-bootstrap | Preserve its corpus and stage history until explicit migration. |
| Bootstrap specification contains five titles | Treat extraction and challenge as planned unless substantive persisted evidence satisfies their gates. |

## 7. Follow-through

Use subsequent domain executions to assess selection usefulness, source-access friction, capability coverage and defects caught by challenge research. Record findings before proposing further family changes. Do not infer research quality from file count, book count, commit count or a maturity label alone.
